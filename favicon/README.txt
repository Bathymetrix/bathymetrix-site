Bathymetrix favicon

The icon is the original lowercase b outline isolated from the black vector wordmark, on a transparent background.

Files:
- favicon.svg: scalable vector favicon
- favicon.ico: multi-resolution 16x16, 32x32, and 48x48 ICO
- favicon-16x16.png, favicon-32x32.png, favicon-48x48.png: PNG fallbacks

Example HTML:
<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="/favicon.svg">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="any">
